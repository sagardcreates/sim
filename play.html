      How to play

      • Move with WASD / arrow keys, or click the ground. Right-drag turns the camera; the wheel zooms.
      • Read people. Icons over heads show what they want: 🍖 hungry, 🍼 children hungry, 🩹 wounded, 🤒 unwell, 🗡️ wants revenge, 😞 unhappy in their clan, 💬 lonely, 💞 seeks a partner, 👑 leader, ⭐ has a following. Click someone to see what they are doing and why.
      • Help them when you are close (inner ring): talk, give food, tend wounds, or back their grudge. Each builds real affection and respect toward you.
      • Invite them to your clan. They decide the way everyone here does, weighing their ties to you and your people against their ties at home. Lonely, unhappy and hungry people come easiest, and their partners and children may follow.
      • Be careful who sees. Anyone of their clan inside the red ring notices you courting their people; their leader counts most. When a clan's suspicion fills, they come at night and kill five of your people. Suspicion fades with time.
      • Food: gather it (G) or take it from your camp's store (F), which your people fill.
      • Raid (R) once you have five grown fighters: steal a clan's store, and pay in blood. Every killing leaves grudges, and grudges lead to feuds.
      • Goal: make your clan the largest in the valley.
